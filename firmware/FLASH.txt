ESP32-S3 Conner pebble — flash from this folder

python3 -m esptool --chip esp32s3 --port /dev/cu.usbmodem14501 -b 460800 \
  --before default_reset --after hard_reset write_flash \
  --flash_mode dio --flash_size 16MB --flash_freq 80m \
  0x0      bootloader.bin \
  0x8000   partition-table.bin \
  0x10000  conner_pebble.bin

If the port is different, replace /dev/cu.usbmodem14501.
